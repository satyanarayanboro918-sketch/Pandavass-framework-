🏹 The Founder's War Council

5 Pandavas

A dharmic hiring framework for early-stage founders 🚀

🦚
Your Role as Krishna

You are not the warrior. You are the charioteer — the one who sees the whole battlefield while everyone else is fighting. Your job is strategy, vision, and preservation of dharma. You don't swing the sword. You decide which sword, when, and toward what.

At ₹1L/month, you cannot hire an army. You hire a war council of five — each a force multiplier. Every wrong hire = one Pandava dead before Kurukshetra. 🎯

₹1,00,000/mo
Keep total salary burn under 50–60% of revenue until you hit ₹3–5L/mo consistently.
₹40–55K salary pool 💰
⬡ The Five — Roles, Duties & Salaries ⬡
I
⚖️
Pandava I · The Righteous
Yudhishthira
Head of Operations & Finance

Keeps the dharma of the business intact — money in, money out, runway, legal compliance, vendor payments. You're busy building; someone must watch the kingdom's treasury.

⚔️ Weapon
Excel / Tally / Zoho Books. Weekly financial dashboards. Zero late invoices or missed GST filings.
Part-time first, full-time after ₹3L/mo
₹6–10K
II
💪
Pandava II · The Destroyer
Bhima
Sales & Business Development

Bhima doesn't strategize. Bhima shows up. Cold-calls, walks into shops, follows up 7 times without shame, closes deals. Hunger to sell must be in their DNA. Bhima carries the revenue weight of the entire army.

⚔️ Weapon
Phone + legs + Google Maps. Local language fluency essential. Base + 10–15% commission per closed client.
Base + aggressive commission 🔥
₹8–12K
III
🎯
Pandava III · The Greatest
Arjuna
Lead Developer / Technical Builder

Sees only the eye of the fish — builds websites, automations, client deliverables. Without Arjuna, everything is strategy with no arrow. Your sharpest, most critical hire of all five.

⚔️ Weapon
Your stack — web dev, n8n, LLM integrations. Self-sufficient, low-maintenance, output-obsessed.
Highest paid — guard like Draupadi 👑
₹12–18K
IV
🎨
Pandava IV · The Beautiful
Nakula
Design & Client Experience

Makes things beautiful. UI polish, social media graphics, proposal decks, client onboarding experience. In WaaS, perception is half the product. Nakula makes clients say "wow" before they even use what they paid for.

⚔️ Weapon
Figma, Canva Pro, basic video editing. Aesthetic sense for Northeast India market positioning.
Can be freelance / per-project initially
₹6–8K
V
🔮
Pandava V · The Wisest
Sahadeva
Growth & Content / Marketing

Knew the future — understood patterns. Manages SEO, social presence, content strategy. Crafts the narrative that makes your brand magnetic to local businesses. Slow to show results, lethal at scale. This hire compounds over time.

⚔️ Weapon
Instagram, WhatsApp Business, Google My Business for clients. Regional language content = massive moat.
Hire last — intern-to-hire works perfectly
₹4–7K
⬡ The Sequence of War — Hire in THIS Order ⬡
1
Phase 1 · Now · ₹1L/mo
💪 Hire Bhima first. Always.
Revenue is oxygen. No Bhima = slow death. Even if rough around the edges, hunger to sell is worth 3 polished people who can't close. Commission structure protects you from fixed burn.
2
Phase 2 · ₹1.5–2L/mo
🎯 Hire Arjuna — the technical executor.
Once you have 3–5 clients, stop building yourself. Arjuna frees you to be Krishna. This hire scales you from 5 to 20 clients without you burning out.
3
Phase 3 · ₹2.5–3L/mo
⚖️ Hire Yudhishthira — the operations keeper.
Real money is moving, real chaos needs managing. GST, invoices, contracts, renewals — Yudhishthira stops the leaks you're too busy to notice. A part-time CA student works great here.
4
Phase 4 · ₹3–4L/mo
🎨 Bring in Nakula — the perception architect.
Now invest in premium positioning. Better client decks, polished brand assets, social proof. Nakula raises your average contract value because you look worth more.
5
Phase 5 · ₹5L+/mo
🔮 Sahadeva enters — the compound machine.
Content and inbound only pay off when you can sustain the investment. Sahadeva builds the flywheel that makes Bhima's job easier — clients come to you. That's when Kurukshetra is truly won.
⚡ Krishna's Laws — What You Must Never Forget
  • I
    You are the charioteer, not the warrior. The moment you stop strategizing and start executing like an employee, you lose the battle. Your one job: see the whole battlefield.
  • II
    A Pandava with wrong dharma is worse than no Pandava. Karna was skilled but fought for the wrong side. Hire for values alignment first, skill second — in a small team, one bad actor poisons everything.
  • III
    Never give equity at this stage. ₹1L/month is too early. Salary + commission + clear promotion path. Equity only when you know they'd die on the battlefield for you.
  • IV
    Protect Arjuna obsessively. Your technical builder is your most irreplaceable asset. If Arjuna leaves, delivery collapses overnight. Keep them challenged and paid above market.
  • V
    Mahabharata was won by dharma, not numbers. Kauravas had more warriors. Win on culture, speed, and knowing your local terrain — Northeast India — better than anyone else ever will.
The Pandavas won Kurukshetra. So will you. 🙏
